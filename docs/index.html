A LITTLE HOW-TO

Find your happy match.

  1. Pick two neighbors. Tap or click a jelly, then one beside it. You can also swipe a jelly toward its neighbor.
  2. Make a line of 3 or more. Match the same shape across or down. A swap that makes no match goes back and costs no move.
  3. Let the jellies tumble. New pieces drop in. Chain reactions multiply your points, all in one move.

You have 20 moves. Each matched jelly earns 10 points, multiplied by the chain reaction number. No moves available? We’ll mix the board for you.

Keyboard: use arrow keys to explore the board, and Enter or Space to select two neighbors. Escape clears your selection.
